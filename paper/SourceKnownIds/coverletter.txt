This manuscript presents a security-oriented architecture for trustworthy distributed-data handling at the identifier level in advanced distributed applications. It separates compact persistence keys, authenticated internal identifiers, and confidential external identifiers through deterministic transformations of one entity identity. The contribution concerns the cross-tier contract and its generation and parsing mechanisms, including collision-guard and backward-verification procedures. Specification and threat analysis, implementation tests, and microbenchmarks evaluate the design under explicit deployment and cryptographic assumptions.
